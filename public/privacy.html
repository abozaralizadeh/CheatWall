What we keep

The topic you type, the level you pick, the poster we make, and when we made it. We keep a poster for 30 days, then delete the poster and its files. We keep only the topic for up to 60 more days, then delete that too.

What we do not keep

No accounts, no names, no email addresses, no tracking cookies and no ads. We do not store your IP address. To enforce the daily limit we turn your IP address into a scrambled code that changes every day, and we store only that code.
