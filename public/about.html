About CheatWall

CheatWall uses AI to create big, beautiful cheat sheet posters for every topic you want to study.

How it works

You type a topic. An AI model writes the cheat sheet. Another pass checks it. Then we lay it out as a poster and give you a PDF you can print and a PNG you can look at.

Please check the facts

AI makes mistakes. Every poster says so. Check anything important before you rely on it, above all for health, law and money topics, where a poster is study material and never advice.

Limits

CheatWall is free for now. Each person can make a few posters a day, and the site has a daily spending limit, so it can be full. Posters are deleted after 30 days.
